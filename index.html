02 What spins in

Five modules. One trip.

Not a listicle. A tidy app on your phone that knows what you like, what you hate and what’s open, built from parts that lock together.

  1. Holla Holla

    One button shows only what’s open right now, in the destination’s own time zone. Works on every screen.

  2. Surprise me

    Pick a vibe, how long you have, walk or ride. Get a mini itinerary from places that are actually open.

  3. Walking ring map

    See what’s within five to thirty minutes of you, with your route drawn stop to stop.

  4. Passport & journal

    Mark places visited, collect stamps, rate with hearts and keep a memory. All stored on your device.

  5. Your own colors

    Generic, city palette or any accent you like. Light, dark, or switch after sunset.
