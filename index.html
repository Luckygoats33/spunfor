02 What’s inside

Five modules. One trip.

Not a listicle. A tidy app on your phone that knows what you like, what you hate and what’s open, built from parts that lock together.

  1. Holla Holla

    One button shows only what’s open right now, in the destination’s own time zone. Works on every screen.

  2. Surprise me

    Pick a vibe, how long you have, walk or ride. Get a mini itinerary from places that are actually open.

  3. Walking ring map

    See what’s within five to thirty minutes of you, with your route drawn stop to stop.

  4. Passport & journal

    Mark places visited, collect stamps, rate with hearts and keep a memory. All stored on your device.

  5. Your own colors

    Generic, city palette or any accent you like. Light, dark, or switch after sunset.

04 How it works

Three quick steps. About two minutes.

  1. 1

    Tell us about the trip

    Flight, dates and where you’re going.

  2. 2

    Share your taste

    What you love, what you’d rather skip, plus budget, group and needs.

  3. 3

    Preview, then unlock

    You get a starter plan built from your answers: a map, a day-by-day frame, guardrails and a checklist. Real venue data is still to come, and New Orleans has a full demo.

Not sure yet? Three spins pick a destination, a vibe and who’s going. You can override or type anything, and it only fills in the form below.
