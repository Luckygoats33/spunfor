      FAQ

      Straight answers.

      Is it really free?

      Yes, while it's in beta. No card, no account.

      Where does the info come from?

      OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons photos (credited on every photo) and Open-Meteo weather. Hours come from OpenStreetMap and can be out of date, so every place says where its hours came from. Check before you go.

      Is my data private?

      Your trip lives in the link and in your phone's browser. No account, no analytics, no cookies. GPS is only used while the app is open, and your location only leaves your phone when you tap Send my location.

      Can my travel partner see what I save?

      Send them your link and they get the same trip and plan. Saves and check-ins stay on each phone for now; live shared lists are coming.

      Which cities?

      Fully curated cities have hand-checked places, photos and hours; the list grows every day as our collector works through every US city and town. Anywhere else, type it in and the app builds itself live from map data the first time you open it. Each city shows whether it is curated or live before you build.
